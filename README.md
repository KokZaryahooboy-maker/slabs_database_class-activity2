# SuperMart SA - My Assignment
Date: 19-10-2026
Course: Database Design

## 1. TPS
TPS is for daily jobs.

At tills:
- scan items
- take money
- print slip
- do returns

At warehouse:
- stock in and out
- damaged stock
- order when low

It must be fast.

## 2. MIS and DSS

MIS - weekly sales:
- how much we sold
- did we reach target
- best and worst items
- what is finished

DSS - when sales drop:
- why did it drop?
- what if we put sale 10% off?
- check old sales if it worked
- tell us what to do

MIS shows problem, DSS helps fix it.

## 3. ESS for CEO
For CEO only. Big picture only.

- profit for 5 years
- what Shoprite does
- what will happen in next 3 years

Just graphs.

## 4. KWS and OAS

KWS - make new things:
- CAD for shop plan
- Power BI to choose new shop place

OAS - office work:
- Outlook and Teams
- Word and Excel
- payroll
